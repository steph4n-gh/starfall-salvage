STARFALL SALVAGE 1.1
Native player download | Local release draft | Prepared 2026-10-14

Thrust, drift and brake through a damaged orbital station. Restore its
systems, protect the crew and bring the salvage home. Story offers 18
missions across six zones and 72 rooms. Arcade repeats a 12-map route
with changing hazard pressure, replacement pulse rigs and banking choices.

COMPATIBILITY AT A GLANCE
Game Boy Color only. Original Game Boy and Game Boy Pocket are unsupported.
ROM: 1 MiB. Save RAM: 32 KiB. Cartridge header: 0x1E, MBC5 with rumble,
RAM and battery. Confirm support for this exact image in your emulator or
cartridge. The .gbc file is not a Game Boy Advance (.gba) game.

The released native ROM completed Story and the focused Arcade/menu tests
in PyBoy 2.7.0. Physical handheld/cartridge combinations, cold-start save
persistence, heard audio and a complete browser campaign remain unverified.
Full details are in RELEASE-NOTES.txt. A listed installation route is not
a claim that every device or cartridge in that category has been tested.
