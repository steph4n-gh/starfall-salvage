STARFALL SALVAGE 1.2
Native player download | Public release | Published 2026-10-14

Thrust, drift and brake through a damaged orbital station. Restore its
systems, protect the crew and bring the salvage home. Story offers 18
missions across six zones and 72 rooms. Arcade repeats a 12-map route
with changing hazard pressure, replacement pulse rigs and banking choices.

COMPATIBILITY AT A GLANCE
Game Boy Color only. Original Game Boy and Game Boy Pocket are unsupported.
ROM: 1 MiB. Save RAM: 32 KiB. Cartridge header: 0x1E, MBC5 with rumble,
RAM and battery. Confirm support for this exact image in your emulator or
cartridge. The .gbc file is not a Game Boy Advance (.gba) game.

Version 1.2 adds filled menu selections, direct continuation from Story
and Arcade dock cards, optional Change Rig choices, objective reminders
and an animated title with its own theme. Movement, combat and objectives
retain their existing rules. The native 1.2 Story pass completed all
72 rooms, 18 guardians and seven rescues (31:26, Rank B). All 12 native
Arcade sectors passed without retry, banking 22,500; same-session Continue
then restored the Story ending. The fourteen-page illustrated manual and
its nine native screenshots were reviewed. Targeted browser checks covered
eight Story rooms and one Arcade sector; banking 300 and earned Story
progress survived ordinary reload. This is not a full browser campaign or
complete Arcade-cycle test.
The owner confirmed 1.2 play on a Chromatic using an SD flash cartridge.
See RELEASE-NOTES.txt for exact scope. Independent hardware validation,
other setups, native cold-start saves, power-off saves and heard audio
remain separate checks.

UPGRADING AN EXISTING SAVE
Back up your old ROM and battery save first. Use the emulator or
cartridge's normal save import/export or association process to connect a
COPY of that save with Starfall-Salvage-1.2.gbc. Some systems match saves by
ROM basename, so changing the ROM filename does not automatically migrate
progress. Keep the original backup until Continue restores the expected
checkpoint and the new version saves and reopens successfully.
