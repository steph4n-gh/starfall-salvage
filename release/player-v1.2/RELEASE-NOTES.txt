STARFALL SALVAGE 1.2 - RELEASE NOTES
Public player release published 2026-10-14

THE GAME
- Story: 18 missions, six zones, 72 large scrolling rooms.
- Salvage, ordered relays, repair, physical crew escorts and guardians.
- Four pulse rigs, middle-route order choices and optional caches.
- Arcade: repeating 12-map route, changing hazard pressure and banking.

VERSION 1.2: CLEARER CHOICES, FEWER STOPS
- Filled cyan selection cards, pointer, centered headings and A/B badges.
- Arcade defaults to FLY ON. One confirmation starts the next sector with
  the current rig. CHANGE RIG is optional; Keep Current is its default.
  A fits the selected rig and flies. B returns to the dock unchanged.
- BANK HAUL secures the score and ends the sortie. B at the dock opens exit
  options; it does not bank the haul.
- Story route choices and ordinary Bay Secured cards launch directly with
  A. Mission Complete opens Flight Deck; choose the next job and A launches.
  The deck initially highlights an available unfinished mission.
- Flight Deck, next-bay and Arcade dock cards show the upcoming objective.
  Pause repeats the current objective. Initial Arcade, retry and restored
  launch checkpoints still have a Ready to Fly briefing.
- Rig descriptions explain the tradeoff in plain words. Rigs replace one
  another; Hull Patch still repairs the hull and returns to Standard.
- Animated ship, exhaust and stars accompany the title theme. There is
  no intro to wait through.
- Confirming a launch does not fire a pulse. Release and press A again
  to pulse. Held directions and B work when play resumes.

Movement, combat, hazards, objective rules, scoring and rig effects retain
their existing design. This update changes presentation and menu flow.

UPGRADING A NATIVE SAVE
Back up the existing ROM and battery-save file. Use your emulator or
cartridge's normal save import/export or association process to connect a
COPY of the old battery save with Starfall-Salvage-1.2.gbc. Some setups use
the ROM basename to find its save, so a new ROM filename does not itself
migrate progress. Do not overwrite the only copy of your previous save.

Continue restores the committed checkpoint stage, including a room-entry
briefing where applicable. Check that the expected progress is present,
then verify saving and reopening on your setup before retiring the backup.
Browser saves belong to the browser and site address; they do not sync
across devices. Native save files and browser storage are managed by
their respective emulator or runtime.
