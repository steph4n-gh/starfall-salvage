The same-origin 1.1-to-1.2 checkpoint upgrade and ordinary-reload route/completed-mission recovery passed. Optional rigs, held-input direct launch, Pause, Retry and exits were exercised. These checks do not establish a full 1.2 browser campaign or complete Arcade cycle.
- Source/host checks cover the revised menu flow, legacy checkpoint stages and unchanged gameplay audio traces. Actual title/six-zone music and effect listening remains unverified.
- Updated player text, eleven exact-native-ROM captures and the fourteen-page manual have been reviewed. All nine embedded gameplay images match their canonical capture pixels. The player archive and generated site are bound by the release manifest.
- The manifest, sync/check commands, failure tests and check-only CI provide the existing consistency workflow. The earlier reviewed 1.1 artifacts and receipts remain historical; they cannot be relabeled as 1.2 evidence.

## Remaining validation, licensing and support work

1. **Blind player tests:** watch 3–5 new players without coaching. Track first dock, brake/pulse/menu understanding, crew proximity, guardian windows, difficulty spikes and reasons for stopping. Measure real human completion times before advertising them.
2. **Additional hardware, audio and saves:** retain the owner's confirmed 1.2 Chromatic/SD-cartridge play. Identify the exact cartridge model and independently check any additional hardware combinations being recommended, crowded scenes and display readability. Title/zone/effect listening on speakers and headphones, and save → complete power off → Continue remain separate checks. The owner report does not establish those results or an exhaustive playthrough. Native cold-boot SRAM testing is unsupported by the current harness; same-session Continue is a narrower check.
3. **Browser scope:** qualify additional supported browsers and recheck delivery after future host or origin changes. Full browser-process termination, storage eviction, cross-origin behavior and cross-device persistence are not established. Browser saves do not sync across devices. Complete a 1.2 browser campaign or full Arcade cycle before claiming those results; the accepted targeted checks may be described with their limits.
4. **Licensing and asset provenance:** public publication is authorized, while original-contribution reuse terms remain unspecified. Public source visibility does not grant a new MIT or other open-source license. The six retained starter files match the installed plugin; [third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md) records their paths, hashes and component notices. Preserve those notices and keep the component scopes clear; see [licensing status](LICENSING.md).
5. **Launch/support:** collect bug reports and feedback through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/steph4n-gh/starfall-salvage/issues), prepare a genuine gameplay clip, recheck all links and publish only claims supported by completed tests. The native 1.2 guided Story clear is 31:26. The older native 35:27 and browser 42:41 results belong to 1.1. None of these successful-room timers measures typical-player duration.
6. **Build qualification:** the [clean-clone comparison](CLEAN-BUILD.json) covers 1.1 on the recorded macOS arm64 toolchain. Repeat the appropriate comparison for 1.2 and qualify additional host/toolchain combinations before claiming support. Preserve the documented signature nondeterminism and the short-temporary-path workaround.
7. **Delivery maintenance:** retain the completed public release's URLs and delivery receipts. Recheck actual audience, anonymous access and exact downloads after future deployments. Reuse the existing Sites deployment; this workflow does not require a separate GitHub Pages pipeline.

Documentation-only changes do not create a newly qualified game build. Keep native gameplay, browser gameplay, source/APU traces, heard audio, physical hardware, persistence and human feedback as separately supported claims.
