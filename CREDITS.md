# Starfall Salvage - Credits

Game by Stephan Arrington, with OpenAI Codex assistance for design, the eighteen-mission Story campaign, Arcade mode, native implementation, original pixel artwork, six-zone chiptune composition and sound effects, and instruction booklet.

Version 1.2 adds the animated native title artwork, the original title theme **“Ignition Vector”**, and revised menus and room-transition presentation. The accepted [native Story and Arcade runs](docs/NATIVE-VERIFICATION.json) and [targeted browser checks](docs/BROWSER-VERIFICATION.json) have separate scopes. Audio source and register checks do not establish speaker/headphone listening, which remains unverified; see [verification scope](docs/VERIFICATION.md).

Illustrated box art was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The original prompt and provenance are retained in [BOX-ART-PROMPT.md](BOX-ART-PROMPT.md). The illustration is not a native game frame. Gameplay screenshots require separate exact-ROM capture provenance.

[GB Studio](https://www.gbstudio.dev/) by Chris Maltby and contributors; [GBDK-2020](https://gbdk.org/) by its contributors. The official browser export includes the Binjgb emulator and its retained license notice. The booklet was prepared with ReportLab.
